## Brand & Style

The design system reimagines high-scale commerce through the lens of a nocturnal expedition. Rejecting generic corporate blues and generic white retail canvas, this interface casts the catalog into a technical, high-contrast dark forest biome. The personality is deliberate, architectural, disciplined, and razor-sharp. It fuses brutalist precision—exemplified by flat planes, zero-radius geometry, and technical typography—with deep equatorial earth tones.

The audience consists of demanding tastemakers, collectors, and executive catalog administrators who value information density, instantaneous visual orientation, and strict utilitarian elegance over decorative softness. Every surface asserts stability; every boundary is cut with surgical 90-degree discipline. The emotional tone evokes stepping into an architectural night-station: quiet, tactical, unhurried, yet primed for rapid high-throughput operations.

## Colors

The palette transforms the savannah dusk into a deep nocturnal administrative environment. The interface operates natively in dark mode, anchored by deep forest charcoal, weathered volcanic slate, and sun-baked earthen pigments.

- **Primary (`#1A1F16`):** Deep Forest Charcoal. Forms the principal foundation for canvas regions, structural framing, persistent sidebars, and elevated component bodies.
- **Secondary (`#E07A5F`):** Terracotta Sunset. The high-contrast interactive trigger. Used sparingly for critical conversion triggers, active state rules, focused borders, and dynamic badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Savannah Amber. The technical highlight channel. Governs telemetry flags, star ratings, system alerts, inventory warnings, and secondary metric calls.
- **Neutral (`#141812`):** Deep Pitch Slate. The root background floor beneath all modular surfaces, creating spatial separation when juxtaposed against container panels.

### Contrast & Support Tones
- **Surface Elevation High (`#242C1F`):** Interactive hover states, active row selections, and highlighted table cells.
- **Surface Elevation Higher (`#2E3827`):** Focused drawers, dropdown overlays, and modal backgrounds.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp alabaster white for headlines, numeric prices, and high-priority entity metrics.
- **Text Muted (`#9CA3AF`):** Neutral gray-slate for metadata, SKU indicators, table headers, and inactive navigational routes.
- **Hairline Structural Borders (`#2C3424` / `#3B4A3F`):** Crisp single-pixel strokes establishing architectural cell walls and module bounds without relying on ambient blurs.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural, mechanical energy of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible engineering precision of **Geist**.

### Hierarchy & Function
- **Space Grotesk (Headlines, Labels, Prices):** Enforces a technical, architectural tone across hero statements, product titles, category flags, and transactional pricing. All micro-labels and category tags are set in uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded letter spacing (`0.08em`) to guarantee quick optical parsing in low-light environments.
- **Geist (Body, Data Grids, Forms):** Delivers uncompromised legibility for catalog specifications, dense administrative tables, form fields, and long descriptions. Geist's neutral proportions balance the geometric idiosyncrasies of Space Grotesk.
- **Tabular Numerals:** All pricing (`price-display`) and inventory tallies enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain strict vertical alignment in checkout receipts, cart breakdowns, and data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a rigid 12-column architectural grid with flush, edge-to-edge alignment patterns. Components do not float ambiguously; they interlock along defined vertical and horizontal planes.

### Grid & Viewport Scaling
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column grid, max container width of 1440px with 32px (`margin`) outer gutters. Storefront catalog displays in 4 columns (`gutter: 1.5rem`). Administrative views run fluid with a fixed 260px tactical sidebar.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins. Catalog items scale to 2 or 3 columns. The administrative sidebar collapses into an edge-docked utility rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`margin-mobile`). Catalog views collapse into 1 or 2 high-density stacked cards. Administrative modules rely on swipeable full-bleed panels.

### Structural Spacing Scale
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between label tags, input icons, and indicator dots.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between list elements, table row cell insets, and compact button padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for cards, input groups, and standard component margins.
- `space-lg` (24px): Structural breaks between form rows, table headers, and split-screen columns.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section divisions, promotional spotlight banners, and major module separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through tonal zoning and structural outlines rather than soft, blurred drop-shadows. The nocturnal forest aesthetic repudiates diffusion, opting instead for architectural stacking.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#141812`):** Ground level. Holds viewport gutters, body backgrounds, and table gutters.
- **Level 1 Containers (`#1A1F16`):** Primary product cards, navigation bars, admin table bodies, and static sidebar frames. Differentiated from the base by a 1px solid stroke in `#2C3424`.
- **Level 2 Floating Surfaces (`#242C1F`):** Quick-view drawers, cart slide-outs, modal dialogs, and active tooltips. Outlined with a sharper 1px border in `#3B4A3F`.
- **Active / Focused Layer (`#2E3827`):** Hovered product cards, active menu tiers, and selected table rows.

### Hard Offset Accents
When an element must project dramatic elevation (such as a promotional dialog or urgent toast alert), use a brutalist hard offset shadow:
`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #0E120D, 0 0 0 1px #3B4A3F`.
This delivers immediate physical separation without introducing blurred artifacts.

## Shapes

The shape grammar is absolute: `roundedness: 0`. 

Every interface boundary is cut at a razor 90-degree angle. Buttons, input fields, image viewports, drawer containers, badges, and modals have zero border-radius. This geometry communicates functional engineering, high density, and architectural authority. 

Pills and soft radiuses are explicitly disallowed. High-contrast indicators and status tags rely on square proportions, tight letter spacing, and hard framing borders to differentiate themselves from ambient content.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Sharp rectangular frame (0px radius).
- **Primary Action (Terracotta Sunset):** Solid `#E07A5F` fill, pure black `#141812` text, weight 600. Hover: background shifts to `#D46346` with an inner 1px white top highlight. Active: scale 0.99, fill `#B94F35`.
- **Secondary Utility (Deep Forest):** `#1A1F16` background, 1px solid `#3B4A3F` border, `#F8FAFC` text. Hover: `#242C1F` background with `#E07A5F` border stroke.
- **Destructive Action:** Solid `#DC2626` text with `#1A1F16` background and 1px `#DC2626` border. Hover: `#DC2626` background with `#F8FAFC` text.
- **Sizing:** 44px height for storefront primary triggers; 36px height for high-density administrative controls.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Geometry:** 0px radius, 1px perimeter border.
- **Status Tags:** Compact box (height: 20px) with 6px horizontal padding.
- **Sale / Discount:** Solid `#E07A5F` background, `#141812` bold text, uppercase `label-micro`.
- **Stock Indicators:** Solid `#1A1F16` background, 1px `#15803D` border, with `#4ADE80` text.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Ghost box with 1px `#2C3424` border; switches to 1px `#E07A5F` border with `#E07A5F` text when selected.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Form Controls:** 44px storefront height, 36px admin height. Solid `#141812` background, 1px solid `#2C3424` border, `#F8FAFC` value text, `#9CA3AF` placeholder.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#E07A5F`, accompanied by a hard 2px solid outer ring `#E07A5F` with 0px offset. No blurred glows.
- **Error State:** 1px solid `#DC2626` with red validation message set in Geist 12px underneath the bounding box.
- **Add-on Groups:** Currency prefixes (`$`) and unit selectors are bound into sharp `#1A1F16` rectangular blocks locked flush to the input edge.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Geometry:** Exact 18x18px square (0px radius) with 1.5px solid `#3B4A3F` border.
- **Checked State:** Solid `#E07A5F` fill with a sharp geometric black checkmark or centered inner square (for radios).
- **Focus:** Sharp 2px offset border in `#F8FAFC`.

### Product & Admin Cards
- **Product Storefront Card:**
  - Base: `#1A1F16` background framed in 1px `#2C3424`.
  - Image Frame: Aspect ratio 1:1, full-bleed to top and sides, separated from details by a 1px bottom border in `#2C3424`.
  - Content Block: 16px internal padding. Category label in uppercase Space Grotesk 11px `#9CA3AF`; product title in bold 18px `#F8FAFC`; dual price display with strikethrough in `#6B7280` and current price in `#E07A5F`.
  - Hover Interaction: Card border shifts from `#2C3424` to `#E07A5F`; quick-action bottom rail slides up flush.
- **Admin Metric Card:**
  - Base: `#1A1F16` background with a 3px solid top border in `#D97706` (Amber) or `#E07A5F` (Terracotta).
  - Metrics: Massive 32px tabular digits in `#F8FAFC` with muted trailing trend indicators (+14.2% in `#15803D`).

### Tables & Data Grids
- **Header Row:** Height 40px, background `#141812`, 1px solid bottom border in `#3B4A3F`. Text in uppercase `label-micro` `#9CA3AF`.
- **Data Rows:** Alternating row striping between `#1A1F16` and `#1E241A`. 1px horizontal borders in `#242C1F`.
- **Hover State:** Row switches instantly to `#2E3827` with an active 2px left border strip in `#E07A5F`.
- **Numerics:** Tabular right-aligned values for SKUs, inventory counts, and currencies.

### Drawers & Slide-outs
- **Cart & Entity Editors:** Full-height, 0px radius panel sliding from the right viewport edge.
- **Styling:** `#1A1F16` body with a persistent 1px left border in `#3B4A3F`. Fixed header and footer action bars with edge-to-edge full-width action buttons.
